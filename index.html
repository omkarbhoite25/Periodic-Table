Elements for space

Periodic Table for Space

A periodic table for mission planning: where each element came from, where we can find it beyond Earth, and what it does aboard a spacecraft. Claims that have not been demonstrated are labelled speculative.

Sources. Standard atomic weights: IUPAC/CIAAW abridged and conventional values; a bracketed value is the mass number of the longest-lived isotope. Abundances: Lodders (2003, 2021), log10 atoms per 106 silicon atoms, ±0.1 dex. Origins: after J. A. Johnson, Science 363, 474 (2019). Electron configurations: NIST ground states; Z ≥ 104 predicted. Mission facts: NASA, ESA, JAXA and CNSA mission records.

How to read the labels. Flown means demonstrated in space. Ground test means demonstrated only on Earth. Planned or in development means funded or designed but not flown. Speculative means not demonstrated and not committed; treat it as an idea, not a capability. M-type asteroid compositions are inferred from iron meteorites; no M-type has been sampled.

Supply-risk scores are an editorial heuristic for comparison only, not an official criticality assessment.
